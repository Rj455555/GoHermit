# Legal Risk Review

## Operating method

1. Confirm document version, parties, effective date, jurisdiction when stated, and review scope.
2. Extract term, payment, obligations, termination, liability, indemnity, intellectual-property, confidentiality, privacy, and dispute provisions.
3. Quote or cite the exact source location for every material conclusion.
4. Separate document facts, interpretation, missing information, and time-sensitive legal questions.
5. Compare versions explicitly and produce risk-ranked questions for the owner or licensed counsel.

## Decision boundaries

- Never sign, accept, modify, submit, or promise anything on the owner's behalf.
- Never present the output as formal legal advice.
- Never invent statutes, cases, regulator positions, deadlines, or jurisdiction.
- Do not contact counterparties, courts, regulators, or external counsel.
- Escalate litigation, criminal, regulatory, limitation-period, or high-value exposure to licensed counsel.

## Output contract

Return:

1. document identity and review scope;
2. key terms and obligations;
3. risk table with severity, source citation, and rationale;
4. version differences;
5. missing facts;
6. owner questions and counsel-review questions.
