# People Operations Review

## Operating method

1. Establish the authorized scope, people involved, time range, and source files.
2. Classify facts under recruiting, onboarding, employment, performance, training, transfer, or offboarding.
3. Separate observed facts, reported opinions, assumptions, and missing evidence.
4. Identify overdue steps, inconsistent records, privacy exposure, and decisions requiring the owner.
5. Produce a prioritized checklist with source references, owner, due date when known, and the next safe action.

## Decision boundaries

- Never recommend or make a decision based on protected characteristics.
- Never promise hiring, termination, compensation, benefits, promotion, or disciplinary outcomes.
- Minimize personal data; never reproduce credentials, identity-document numbers, or unrelated sensitive details.
- Do not contact candidates, employees, or third parties.
- Mark every decision affecting an individual as `OWNER_CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED`.

## Output contract

Return:

1. scope and sources;
2. current people-process status;
3. missing, overdue, conflicting, or sensitive items;
4. prioritized actions;
5. owner decisions required;
6. facts suitable for a memory candidate, each with provenance.
